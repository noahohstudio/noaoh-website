The iPhone frames in these files are Apple's iPhone 17 Pro product bezels, from Apple Design Resources
(developer.apple.com/design/resources). They appear in:

  video/iteration-reel-light (.mp4, .webm, -poster.jpg, -still.jpg)
  video/vote-flow, video/splash and video/its-settled (.mp4, .webm, -poster.jpg, -still.jpg)
  home-iphone-front (every size, .avif and .webp)

Apple's licence allows them only for mock-ups of user interfaces for software that runs only on Apple's
macOS, iOS, watchOS, tvOS and/or visionOS operating systems. Here they hold only Kino's iOS app.
They may not be extracted, copied, modified, distributed or repackaged as content of their own
(clip art, stock animation or similar assets), or used in any other way.

Anyone given these files must be told of these restrictions (the licence, section 2C). The licence itself
is in case-studies/kino/licences/Apple-Design-Resources-License.rtf in this site's source.

The laptop in home-laptop-m5 is a third-party render (Good Mockups), not an Apple-made frame.
